## 1. Brand Positioning & Strategy

### 1.1 Brand Positioning Statement
> **TRAITIST** is an independent, contemporary streetwear studio engineered for thinkers, researchers, coders, and academic rebels. We transform the existential grind of high-level intellectual pursuit—peer review, nocturnal thesis writing, imposter syndrome, and laboratory perseverance—into heavyweight, uncompromising urban uniform garments.

### 1.2 Core Brand Values
1. **Intellectual Authenticity:** We never patronize or use superficial "nerd" tropes. Our humor and concepts come directly from the frontlines of genuine research and development.
2. **Material Integrity:** 200 GSM heavyweight pure combed cotton jersey, bio-washed and pre-shrunk. Structured drape that maintains its architectural silhouette wash after wash.
3. **Understated Luxury & Restraint:** High visual contrast, confident whitespace, brutalist typography, and subtle tone-on-tone textures. No visual slop, gratuitous badges, or cheap retail desperation.
4. **Resilience & Solidarity:** "Threads of Resilience." Dressing well is an act of quiet defiance against burnout and bureaucratic academic fatigue.

### 1.3 Tone of Voice
- **Tone Profile:** Dry, existential, erudite, self-aware, and quietly confident.
- **Humor Standard:** Grounded in authentic research reality ("Your thesis isn't the only thing that needs a revision", "Questions? We've done the homework", "Zero peer review required").
- **Never:** Sarcastic, childish, condescending, or spammy.

## 2. Visual Identity & Art Direction

### 2.1 The Color Philosophy
| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Canvas** | Architectural Paper | `#ECEBE6` | Primary background. Warm, tactile, non-glaring off-white. |
| **Ink** | Carbon Black | `#111111` | Primary typography, borders, high-contrast brutalist buttons. |
| **Signal Accent** | International Orange | `#FF5722` | Strategic accent, notification pulses, exclusive drop indicators. |
| **Surface** | Cement Tint | `#DFDDD7` | Card backgrounds, drawer panels, subtle elevated surfaces. |
| **Border** | Muted Concrete | `#D1CFCA` | Hairline dividers, table grids, structural frames. |
| **Success** | Terminal Emerald | `#10B981` | Order confirmed, bag item added, verified badges. |

### 2.2 Typography Hierarchy
- **Display Typeface:** `Syne` (Bold / ExtraBold / Heavy, 700–900 weight, -0.04em letter-spacing). Used for primary section titles, hero headlines, and brand impact.
- **Editorial Typeface:** `Newsreader` (Italic / Normal, 400–600 weight). Used for sophisticated storytelling, thesis quotes, and academic citations.
- **Body Typeface:** `Inter` (300–600 weight). Used for high-legibility descriptions, product specs, and UI copy.
- **Technical & Monospace:** `Space Grotesk` (500–700 weight, uppercase, tracking-wider). Used for labels, SKUs, pricing, metadata, and timecodes.

## 3. Product Presentation Standards

1. **Garment Mockups:**
   - True-to-life studio photography on transparent backgrounds with multiply/normal blending over `#DFDDD7` surfaces.
   - Clean drop-shoulder boxy drape without synthetic plastic sheen.
2. **Pricing Transparency:**
   - Clear, clean INR pricing (`₹799` Launch Offer, regular `₹899`).
   - Zero hidden fees, zero checkout surprises. Free shipping across India.
3. **Sizing & Specs:**
   - 200 GSM Heavyweight Combed Cotton.
   - Pre-shrunk & bio-washed.
   - Screen-printed high-density archival graphics.
   - Hassle-free 7-day size exchanges.

## 4. Design System Principles

- **Three-Layer Tokens:** Primitive (hex) → Semantic (purpose) → Component (`--btn-primary-bg`).
- **Tactile Physics:** 180ms ease transitions, active button scale `0.975`, smooth Lenis scroll.
- **Mobile First Touch Targets:** Minimum 44×44px interactive tap area on all touch surfaces.
- **Accessibility:** Full WCAG 2.2 Level AA compliance, semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus rings.
